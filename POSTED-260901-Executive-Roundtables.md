For seven years, I sat in a CEO roundtable that shaped how I approach leadership. It wasn’t a troubleshooting group. It was a thinking group. We didn’t bring “problems.” We brought growth constraints and strategic inflection points—what we needed to accomplish next and what stood in the way.

In my VC‑backed role, the quarterly board meetings functioned the same way. The board didn’t try to run the business. They stayed in their lane. But they were exceptional at helping us think clearly and systematically. No formulas. Just disciplined reasoning and very sharp minds.

Across two growth companies—both of which exited successfully—the pattern was the same: structured roundtables matter. Vistage groups, Chamber groups, advisory boards, statutory boards… they all create the governance backbone that makes real operating structures possible.
